Diploma in Electrical Engineering

Diploma in Electrical Engineering offers a wide array of opportunities for career advancement and professional growth. By combining practical skills with theoretical knowledge, this program prepares students to excel in diverse industries. Graduates are well-equipped to pursue roles in government, autonomous, and non-governmental organizations across sectors such as electrical power generation and distribution, telecommunications, and information technology. Additionally, employment prospects extend to organizations like the Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board (BREB), Public Works Department (PWD), Dhaka Electric Supply Company Limited (DESCO), Roads and Highways (R&H), Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB), Military Engineering Service (MES), Dhaka Power Distribution Company (DPDC), and Power Grid Company of Bangladesh (PGCB), among others. The abundance of employment opportunities available to diploma holders in electrical engineering underscores the program's relevance and potential for success in today's dynamic job market.

Admission Eligibility

  • Minimum SSC equivalent from any discipline with minimum GPA: 2.00 .
  • HSC (Science) Students can directly join in 3rd Semester.
  • HSC (Vocational) students can directly join in 4th Semester. (Passing year 2011, GPA: 2.00)

Enrollment Requirements

To enroll in Headway Engineering Institute, you need to fulfill the following requirements:

  • Application Form: Collect the application form from the college campus desk and fill it out by hand.
  • SSC Certificate/Transcript: Provide the original certificate or transcript from the SSC or equivalent exam.
  • Photographs: Submit 3 color passport-sized photos and 2 stamp-sized photos.
