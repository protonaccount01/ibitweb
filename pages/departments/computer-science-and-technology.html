Career Prospects

The career prospects for graduates of the Diploma in Engineering Technology program in Computer Science and Technology in Bangladesh are promising and varied. As technology continues to evolve and expand in the country, there is a growing demand for skilled professionals in various sectors. Here are some potential career paths for graduates:

  • ✓
    Further Studies: Graduates may choose to continue their education by pursuing advanced degrees in computer science, information technology, or related fields to expand their career opportunities.
  • ✓
    Software Development: Graduates can pursue careers as software developers, working on designing, developing, and maintaining software applications for different platforms and industries.
  • ✓
    Network Administration: With expertise in computer networks, graduates can work as network administrators, managing and maintaining computer networks for organizations to ensure smooth communication and data exchange.
  • ✓
    Cybersecurity: As cybersecurity becomes increasingly important, graduates can specialize in protecting systems and networks from cyber threats as cybersecurity analysts or specialists.
  • ✓
    Technical Support and IT Helpdesk: Graduates can provide technical support and assistance to users, troubleshooting issues related to software, hardware, and networking.
  • ✓
    Database Administration: Graduates can pursue roles as database administrators, responsible for designing, managing, and maintaining databases for organizations to store and retrieve data efficiently.
  • ✓
    Web Development: With skills in web technologies, graduates can work as web developers, creating and maintaining websites and web applications for businesses and organizations.
  • ✓
    Quality Assurance and Testing: Graduates can specialize in quality assurance and software testing, ensuring that software applications meet quality standards and function as intended.
  • ✓
    Freelancing and Entrepreneurship: Graduates have the option to work as freelancers, offering their skills in programming, web development, or other areas to clients around the world. They can also start their own tech-related businesses.
  • ✓
    Teaching and Training: Graduates can pursue careers in education by teaching computer science and technology courses at technical institutes or offering training sessions to professionals.

Admission Eligibility

  • Minimum SSC equivalent from any discipline with minimum GPA: 2.00 .
  • HSC (Science) Students can directly join in 3rd Semester.
  • HSC (Vocational) students can directly join in 4th Semester. (Passing year 2011, GPA: 2.00)

Enrollment Requirements

To enroll in Headway Engineering Institute, you need to fulfill the following requirements:

  • Application Form: Collect the application form from the college campus desk and fill it out by hand.
  • SSC Certificate/Transcript: Provide the original certificate or transcript from the SSC or equivalent exam.
  • Photographs: Submit 3 color passport-sized photos and 2 stamp-sized photos.

Duration & Assessment

Diploma in computer science and engineering is a 4-year long program consisting of 8 semesters (1 Semester = 6 months). The final and the midterm exams of each semester are conducted under the supervision of the Bangladesh Technical Education Board (BTEB). All the exams question, answer script checking and final results are published by the BTEB. Besides this, each student has to attend the regular class test, quiz test, and semester final project. After successful completion, a student will receive a Diploma Certificate.
