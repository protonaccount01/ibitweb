IBIT-ISLAMI BANK INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY, SYLHET

Islami Bank Institute of Technology (IBIT), Sylhet is a premier (Govt Approved & BTEB Affiliated) Polytechnic Institute managed by Islami Bank Foundation, dedicated to building highly skilled Engineers in various technical sectors. Established in 1999, IBIT Sylhet is a well-reputed institute for Diploma-in-Engineering education, practical technical training, professional skill development, and leadership excellence.
